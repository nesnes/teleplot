Top menu: how many dashboards

What is missing

The dashboard button shows the name of the dashboard on display and a caret. Closed, it looks the same with one dashboard or with ten: nothing says that others exist (the automatic "Live" one, a sample, one made last week). Each mock-up is shown with one dashboard, two, and twelve; the rest of the island is only sketched.

AA count next to the name recommended

The number of dashboards in a small grey pill after the name, like the counts of the Telemetries and Sources buttons. Nothing with a single dashboard.

Good for
The same convention as the rest of the menu: a number next to a button means "this many inside". Compact, stable width.
Watch out
A bare number has to be learned once: the tooltip says "2 dashboards".

BPosition: "1 / 2"

Which one is shown out of how many, like the pages of a document.

Good for
Unambiguous: nobody reads "1 / 2" as anything else than "there is another one".
Watch out
The position means little: dashboards are named, not ordered. Wider than a count.

CA second line under the name

The name moves up a little and a small line says it in words, the same layout as "thinned" under the data rate.

Good for
Words, no learning; no extra width.
Watch out
Two buttons of the island with a small second line is a lot of fine print, and the name loses its vertical centering when the line appears.

DDots, one per dashboard

The dots of a carousel: one per dashboard, the current one filled. A click on a dot could switch directly. Beyond five, the rest is a "+7".

Good for
Shows "several" and "which one" at a glance, and gives a one-click switch between two or three dashboards.
Watch out
Dots have no names: fine for two, a guessing game for five. Small targets.

EA stack icon before the name

A dashboard icon in front of the name becomes a stack of two sheets when there are several, with the count after the name.

Good for
The icon also tells what this button is (today it is only a name), with or without other dashboards.
Watch out
An icon plus a number for one piece of information; the difference between one sheet and two is subtle at this size.

FPrevious / next arrows

Two arrows around the name appear when there is more than one dashboard: they say "there are others" and switch to them without opening the list.

Good for
The hint is also the fastest way to flip between two dashboards (and a natural place for a keyboard shortcut).
Watch out
Does not say how many. Two more controls in the island, and an order between dashboards that the user never chose.

GTabs when there are few

With two or three dashboards, their names are shown side by side as tabs; with more, the most recent ones and a "+10" that opens the list.

Good for
The only one where the other dashboards are named without opening anything.
Watch out
The island gets much wider, and its width changes when a dashboard is created, renamed or deleted. The biggest change by far.

Recommendation

A: a count after the name, hidden when there is only one dashboard. It follows the convention the menu already has for telemetries and sources, costs a few pixels, and never changes the shape of the island. E is A plus an icon, worth it if you also want the button to say what it is. F is a different feature that happens to give the hint: a good addition later if you often flip between two dashboards.
