Data menu: "thinned" under the rate

The idea

While some stored data is thinned out, the rate moves up a little and the word "thinned" appears under it, smaller. The word and the thinned part of the timeline in the menu share one color, so that opening the menu answers "thinned: where?". Each mock-up shows the button with data flowing, the button paused (amber), and the top of the menu.

  • WidthThe word is narrower than the rate at this size: the button keeps its width, the island does not move when it appears.
  • ChartsCharts tint their thinned part in grey with the word "thinned": the chosen color can be carried there too (the word, or the tint).
  • To decide: which colorAmber is the warning color but already means "paused" and "memory above 70 %" on this very button. A color of its own avoids the clash.

1Amber word, amber hatching

The plain word in small bold amber under the rate. In the menu, the thinned part of the timeline and its note take the same amber.

Good for
Amber is what people expect of a warning; the least decoration.
Watch out
Amber already means "paused" (the whole button) and "memory above 70 %" (the ring). On the paused button the word has to turn dark and loses its color link.

2Violet word, violet hatching recommended

The same layout with a color of its own: violet, used nowhere else in the menu. Word, hatching and note share it.

Good for
One color = one meaning. Green/amber/red stay for the memory level, amber for the pause, blue for the data kept, violet for "thinned".
Watch out
Violet does not say "warning" by itself: it is the word that warns. Violet is also in the palette of the series (a chart line can be violet).

3A filled pill

The word in a small filled pill, the same solid color as the thinned part of the bar (no hatching): the pill is a piece of the bar.

Good for
The strongest link: same shape language, same flat color. Reads well in both themes.
Watch out
The heaviest on the button; a solid second color on the timeline makes it look like two kinds of data rather than "the same data, with less detail".

4A swatch of the hatching and the word

Like a chart legend: a tiny sample of the hatching, then the word. Shown here in orange, between the amber of the pause and the red of the full memory.

Good for
The swatch says "this is what you will see in the menu and on the charts": the link is made by the pattern as well as the color.
Watch out
The swatch is 10 px wide: the hatching is hardly more than two stripes. Orange sits close to both amber and red.

5Keep the blue, hatched

No new color: the thinned part stays a hatched version of the blue of the data, as it is today, and the word under the rate is blue with the same hatched swatch.

Good for
Says exactly what it is: the same data, sparser. Nothing to change in the menu.
Watch out
Blue on a blue rate: the word does not stand out, and it is not a warning color at all.

6A colored rule and small capitals

The rate is underlined by a rule in the thinned color, with the word in small capitals under it: a miniature of the timeline and its caption.

Good for
Tidy, aligned with the rate, and the rule echoes the bar of the menu.
Watch out
The smallest text of the six (about 8 px): at the limit of what is readable.

Recommendation

2: the plain word in a color of its own, with the hatching of the timeline in the same color. It is the only one where the color keeps a single meaning, and the word stays readable on the paused button. If amber matters more to you than that, 1 is the same design in amber. 3 is the one to take if the word must be seen from far.
