# Webapp layout and motion

```
[left dock: one side panel] [workspace: floating top menu + dashboard] [right dock: edit panel]
```

- **Docks** (`.dock`, `style.css`) hold the side panels. Opening or closing a dock slides its width (`--dock-time`, 160 ms) instead of inserting the panel at once, so the dashboard resizes smoothly and nothing jumps. The left dock has a fixed width for all panels (`--side-w`): switching between Telemetries and Sources only fades the content. The panel stays displayed until the dock has closed (`shownPanel` in `main.js`). **Help has its own dock on the right** (`ctx.helpOpen`, `.dock-help`, a little narrower), so it can stay open next to Telemetries or Sources: a message tried from the Help is seen arriving in the Telemetries panel. In edit mode the Help dock sits left of the edit panel.
- While a dock moves, the edit chrome (pills, "+" slots) is refreshed on every frame (`motion()` in `main.js`), so it follows the views.
- **Motion tokens**: `--motion-fast` (120 ms, fades and small moves), `--dock-time`, `--ease-out`. Everything quick on purpose: the aim is to follow what moves, not to wait for it. `prefers-reduced-motion` sets them to 0.
- **Workspace**: scrolls by itself (the docks stay in place, `.panel-container` is one window high). A band of room for the top menu sits at its top, scrolled away at start, see `doc/top-menu.md`.
- **Welcome** (`components/welcome.js`) is what the main area shows until there is a dashboard: three steps (send, watch, arrange), a button to try the sample data, and the state of the connection to the server.
- **Help panel** (`components/panel-help.js`, right dock, a little wider than the Export panel: `.dock-help-wide`; design studies `doc/help-panel-mockups.html` to `-4`, then `doc/help-gallery-mockups.html` and `doc/help-sheet-mockups.html` for the sheets): "What do you want to see?", a gallery of results, plus how Teleplot works, shortcuts and the sample data switch (`components/sample-controls.js`).
    - **Tools**: a search field (titles, descriptions, lines and notes of the tiles, and the concepts; nothing found: the field at the bottom tells what a line is), and under it "I send with", a segmented control (Terminal, Python, C++, Arduino; `HELP_SENDERS`), remembered in `localStorage`: the only place where the language is chosen. Every command of the panel is written for that choice (`helpLine()`).
    - **Tiles** (`HELP_TILES`, in the families of `HELP_FAMILIES`): one per thing Teleplot displays (a curve, a gauge, a timeline, a map, a lidar scan, a picture through its camera, settings, a joystick...), each with a picture (`helpPicture()`, SVG colored by the theme). A tile opens its sheet in place of the gallery ("‹ All" goes back). The sheet is about the text protocol: the title with the picture as a thumbnail, then the messages in order (`examples`, numbered when there are several, "Send all"), each a card: what it does in a sentence, the message large with its parts named under them (`helpParts()`: name, label, time, value, unit, flags; the properties of `3D|` / `2D|` lines grouped with their values, the keys of `ATTR|`, the limits of `TH|`, the fields of `DASH|` and `ACK|`; a name already given by an earlier message of the sheet is not repeated), pointing at a part (or focusing it) says what it accepts (`HELP_PART_TIPS`), then, small and grey, the command of the language with the message in it, and Send / Edit / Copy; what Teleplot made of a sent message comes under its card, and its numbers are nudged a little (`helpVary()`: values, positions, rotations; never names, times, attributes or texts), so that sending again shows a change. Lines a tile does not need are marked optional (`optional: true`), after the ones it needs. Each tile is complete by itself on a fresh page (it never needs what another tile sent; `DASH|` lines come first, so that the next lines show in the view they create), and a line sent again shows a change: numbers are nudged, texts go through their `cycle`. Times in messages are `{now}` (`helpFill()`: the time of the page, refreshed every second), never a fixed date that would squash a chart. After a Send, the dashboard that displays the result comes to the front (`showDashboardOf`: the one of a `DASH|` line, else one with a view of the telemetry, the current one first; not while editing). "More ways" lists the titles of its ways; each has its title. Tiles of Control say when commands are off, with a button to switch them on. `tests/help.test.js` sends each tile alone on a fresh page and checks the views it gives. "Set up, once" (folded) when the language needs it, how it works, more ways (`more`, folded). No related tiles. Tiles of the family "In the page" have no message: their keys and a button that opens the right place (Edit mode, Export, the data menu, Sources, CMD...).
    - **Start here**: with nothing received, a card with the first message and its Send button, above the gallery. No suggestions, progression or "new" badges: they were tried and dropped (the gallery stays the same for everyone).
    - **How Teleplot works** (`HELP_CONCEPTS`): the data flow (device, server, page, commands back), a telemetry, time (the last sample at or before the time shown, never one made up), views and dashboards, space and frames, commands; each a schematic (`helpSchema()`) and a few paragraphs, and related tiles.
    - **Tests**: every line is checked against the library's parser, the lines of a tile are sent in order and give what the tile says, the pictures are drawn, the parts of every message are named and nudged messages are still understood (`tests/help.test.js`). The C++ lines are only the ones its sample shows (`clients/cpp/sample.cpp`); the others say so and show the text line.
    - **Colors**: in every line the name, label, value, time, unit and flags of the message keep one color each (the kinds of `helpParts()`, the one display helper that follows the rules of the parser: anatomy, command lines and the try field all use it).
    - **Try field** at the bottom, always at hand: the message is colored as it is typed and its parts are named above it, Enter or Send gives it to `TP.parseDataText` as if a device had sent it, and `helpDescribe()` says what Teleplot made of it (or what is wrong), above the field. "Edit" on a line puts it there; Esc empties it.
