Feedback on the first implementation: the message was lost among pictures, tabs, buttons and related tiles. These sheets are about the text protocol: the message is the biggest thing, each part is named, and the way to send it from a language comes second, small. The language is chosen once, at the top of the panel. "Related" is gone; the picture shrinks to a thumbnail.

1 · Before and after

Same tile, "A curve". Left: what is implemented today. Right: proposal A (names under the parts), the one recommended.

2 · Naming the parts of a message

Three ways to say what each part is. The colors are the ones of the try field and of every line of the panel, so they are learned once.

3 · The language: chosen once, at the top

One control, always in the same place, under the search. The command of the language is a small line under each message (D), or is explained once per sheet as a template where the message goes (E).

4 · Several lines: "A gauge"

A tile that needs several messages. Each line is a card of its own, numbered, with what it does in one sentence. Parts already explained in an earlier line are not named again (the name), so new parts stand out.

5 · Long lines: shapes

A 3D| or 2D| line has many fields. They are named by property (a property and its values are one part), and the line wraps between parts, never inside one.

6 · Rest, hover, sent

Pointing at a part says what it accepts (what the parser takes). Send gives the message to the page; what Teleplot made of it comes under the card, with an icon.
