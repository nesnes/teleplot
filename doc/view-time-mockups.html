Time of the views: with the dashboard, or apart

What is asked

Today every view of a dashboard shares one zoom and one cursor (one "group" in the library). Two needs are not covered: keeping a view on the latest data while the past is explored elsewhere, and comparing two moments. The library can already do both (any number of groups); the question is how little of it to show.

  • A: a switch per view"Time" among the options of a view: follows the dashboard, always live, and for charts a time of its own. No groups to name.
  • B: a switch per containerThe same choice on a row, column or stack: what is inside shares a time, apart from the rest.
  • Common rulesThe default never changes (everything together). A view or container that is apart says so on screen. Esc and "back to live" release everything.

A1The switch, in the Edit panel of a view

Edit mode, a view selected: one more line among its options. Two choices for values, images and 3D scenes; charts get a third one (A3). Nothing else is added to the editor.

Recommendation

Start with A1 + A2 ("always live" on any view): it answers the most common need, adds one line to the Edit panel and one small tag on screen. A3 (a chart with its own zoom) is a small step more and covers the comparison of two moments for charts. B is the complete answer but needs a frame, a header and a color per container, and containers are not something a newcomer selects: I would keep it for later, built on the same option.
