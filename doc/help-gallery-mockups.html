Proposal 3 of help-panel-mockups-4.html, made concrete. The Help panel (right dock, 30 rem as today) opens on what can be seen: a gallery of results, grouped in families. A tile opens its sheet: the picture large, the lines to send (in the language the user sends with), how it works, more ways, related tiles. Every message and every result shown here exists in Teleplot today; the help tests keep checking that each line is understood.

The panel, step by step

How it looks the first time, and after a while. The grid is the same; what changes is the hero, the checks and the suggestions.

1First open: nothing received yet

A Start here card on top, the first tile already open in it: the line, Send, and a sentence. Below, the whole gallery, so that nothing is hidden; families keep their order from simple to advanced.

  • The sender ("Terminal") is asked by the chip at the top, remembered.
  • The search filters tiles by words of their title, description and lines ("map", "lidar", "button").
  • Bottom: the try bar (one line); above the footer: sample data, shortcuts, documentation.

2After a while: checks and suggestions

The hero is gone (it comes back with "Start again" in the footer). Suggested next: two tiles chosen from what came in, with the reason under them. A count says how much has been discovered. Tiles of features new since the last visit carry new (here: Control).

Rules of suggestion, in order: a number without range → A gauge; a text state → A timeline; 2 or 3 numbers → A map or 3D; an image → Through a camera; many telemetries → Your dashboard; anything for a while → Settings (once).

Sheets

A tile opens its sheet in place of the grid (‹ All goes back, Esc too). Same structure for every tile: picture, lines, how, more, related.

3A gauge, before and after Send

Lines are the steps of a recipe, in order, numbered when there are several. Send sends that line to this page; Send all sends the recipe. The result comes back under the line (the text of helpDescribe today), with Show me: the view that displays it blinks on the dashboard.

The check of the tile and the progress update at once.

4The same sheet, for each sender

The tabs above the lines are the senders. The line becomes what that sender writes: an nc command, a Python call, a C++ call, an Arduino Serial.println. When a sender needs a setup (Python's socket, Arduino's Serial.begin), a "Set up, once" line comes first, folded after the first time.

5Control: what the device sends, what it answers

For commands, lines go both ways: they are tagged device (sends) and page (what your device will receive). A hint reminds that CMD has to be on, with the button to switch it.

6"In the page": no message, a gesture

Tiles about the interface show what to do (keys, where to click) and open the right place: Edit mode, the Export panel, the data menu, the Sources panel. Their picture is the interface, not data.

7Searching

Typing filters the grid as you type; the families of the tiles found stay as headings. Nothing found: the closest words, and the try bar ("Paste a line you have: we tell you what it is").

Variants

ATile density

2 columns (recommended in a 30 rem dock): pictures large enough to recognize, a title and a few words. 3 columns: everything on one screen, but pictures too small to tell a map from a scan. A list: the densest, closest to today, the least visual.

BWhere the sheet opens

In place (recommended): the grid gives way, ‹ All comes back to the same scroll position. Under its row: the grid stays, the sheet pushes the next rows down (good on a tall screen, jumpy). Next to the panel: a sheet floats over the dashboard, the grid stays visible (more room for the picture, hides data).

CPictures: drawn, or live

Drawn (the grid): small illustrations, the same for everyone, cheap. Live (suggested for the sheet): a real view of the library fed with the lines of the recipe, so what is shown is exactly what the user will get, theme included; editing a line in the try bar updates it. This needs a second library instance (the bundle already initializes from a given object: to be confirmed for its update loop and its styles).

DProgressive: suggest, or unfold

Everything visible, suggestions on top (recommended): nothing is hidden, the path is a hint. Unfold: only Numbers and Text at first, the other families as one line each ("Space · 5 results") that open when one basic tile is discovered or on click. Calmer at first, but features can be missed.

EThe try bar

At the bottom (recommended), like the input of a chat: always at hand, never in the way of the gallery; it grows to show what was understood. At the top (today): visible first, but it pushes the gallery down and reads as the main thing to do.

Spec of the tiles

What each tile shows and when it counts as discovered. The rules read the datastore (and the views for timelines and marks), checked once per second while the panel is open; a check is kept (localStorage) once earned.
