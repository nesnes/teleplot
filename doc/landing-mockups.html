The landing page is what the main area shows while there is no dashboard to display: nothing received yet, nothing created. Today it says three steps (send, watch, arrange), offers the sample data and the Help, and tells the state of the server. Each study below is a storyboard: 1 at rest, 2 while something happens (the first message, a server that cannot be reached), 3 after it. Under each, what the device sent for each frame.

What it must tell a newcomer

In the order of the questions of someone who just started the server or opened the page. The first four are what the page is for; the others help, or must stay out.

  • 1. Is it working? mustThe server is reachable and listening; or it is not, and the one thing to do about it. An icon, the port; no wall of text.
  • 2. Where do I send? mustThe address and the port: 127.0.0.1:47269 on this computer, and the address of this computer for a device on the network (the server would have to tell it: its hello message does not today).
  • 3. What do I send? mustOne line, written for the way the user sends data (terminal, Python, C++, Arduino: the choice of the Help), with Copy. name:value is the whole protocol at first.
  • 4. Did it arrive? mustThe page reacts to the first packet: it says what came, and goes to what shows it (the Live dashboard). A packet that was not understood says why.
  • 5. Other ways in shouldA serial port (no server needed, Chrome / Edge), a file (.teleplot, .txt, .csv: drop it), the sample data (nothing to write).
  • 6. What can I get? shouldA glimpse of the results (curves, gauges, maps, 3D, pictures, buttons), each one a way into its Help tile, not a copy of it.
  • 7. When nothing comes shouldAfter a while without packets: the usual causes (wrong port or address, firewall, server on another machine, page opened as a file).
  • 8. Where to learn more couldThe Help (its gallery), the documentation, the shortcuts (H, Space…).
  • Leave outA tour, progress, "new" badges, suggestions (not wanted in the Help either); a copy of the Help's first tile (the box removed from the Help repeated it); marketing text; anything that stays once data flows.

Recommendation

A (one line) as the base: it answers questions 1 to 4 on one screen, for the language the user chose, and goes away by itself when the first packet arrives. Take from B the path drawn small under the line (device, server, page: which one is missing, and the one fix), from E the other ways in as three quiet links under it (serial port, file, sample), and from J the line that tells why a packet was not understood. H (the ghost dashboard) is the most original and the one that teaches the most about where things go; it is a bigger change (the landing becomes the Live dashboard itself) and could be a second step once A is in place. Needed in the server for all of them: its addresses in the hello message (question 2).
