Each source of the Send column of the welcome page (components/welcome.js) shows the way its data takes to this page. The links show what the page knows: solid green where data has passed, dashed grey where nothing came yet, red with a cross where it is cut, plain blue-grey where there is nothing to watch (a file, the sample). Each variant is a storyboard (1 waiting, 2 no server, 3 data) with what the device sent.

Recommendation

A (a line of nodes and labelled links under each title): every card says its way at a glance, the cut is where the problem is, and it uses the words of the protocol (UDP, WebSocket, USB serial) without a paragraph. C is the same in the drawing of the logo (rings and traces), the most in the style of the page; it reads a little less (no words on the links). E teaches the whole picture in one place (the server is only on the way of the network), but adds a box above the cards. D (on hover) keeps the page the calmest and hides the paths from touch screens.
