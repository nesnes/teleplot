The logo, read

The landing page becomes the logo, large: on the left what data can come from, on the right what it became. Each study below is a storyboard (1 at rest, 2 something happens, 3 after) with what the device sent for each frame, as in landing-mockups.html.

Rules for drawing it

  • A ring is a real sourceDrawn only for a way in that exists here (the server's UDP port, the serial port when the browser has Web Serial, files, the sample). Its look is its state: full when it delivers, outlined when ready, grey when it cannot work now.
  • A trace is a real pathPlain when data passes, dashed when waiting, broken (and red) where it is cut: the server cannot be reached, the port is closed.
  • The curve is never inventedBefore data the right side is an empty dashed path, clearly a place, never a curve that looks like data (Teleplot never invents samples). Each red dot is a sample that came, at its value.
  • The drawing hands overWhen data flows the landing goes, and the curve it drew is the first chart of the Live dashboard: the same samples, no jump.
  • Words stay shortWhat a ring is (UDP 47269), its one line to send, and why when it cannot work. The Help holds the rest.
  • Small screensThe drawing turns a quarter: sources on top, curve below. Without the drawing (reduced motion, narrow) the same pieces stay as a list.

Recommendation

2 (the circuit) as the page: its rings are the ways in (question "where from?" answered by the drawing itself), a click on a ring opens its one line next to it, and its traces tell what works (study 6 merged in: a cut trace is a server that cannot be reached). With 1's rule for the right side: the dashed place of the curve fills with the real samples as they come, and 5's hand-over: the curve slides into the first chart of the Live dashboard. 7 (one ring per sender) is the natural next step, once the page knows its senders (the server tells them already). 4 (the curve as a ladder of results) is the one that changes the logo's meaning the most: nice, but it makes the red dots mean something else than samples.
