Data menu: a sign that data was thinned out

A different direction

The first generation tried to stay discreet: marks of a few pixels inside the ring. This one takes the word "warning" at face value: signs that are objects of their own, in the warning color, large enough to be seen. Each is shown without thinning, with thinned data, with the memory nearly full, and while paused (where the button is already amber: the sign turns dark).

AA warning triangle before the rate recommended

The universal sign, small, between the ring and the rate. Hovering it says "Old data of robot.camera was thinned out to fit in memory".

Good for
Nobody has to learn it. It is a separate object: it never muddles the ring.
Watch out
Adds about 20 px to the button. A triangle is a strong word for a policy that works as intended.

HAn amber line at the foot of the control

A short amber bar along the bottom edge of the control, like the mark under an open tab.

Good for
Quiet and tidy, readable in both themes.
Watch out
Abstract: it says "state", not "warning", until the tooltip is read.

Recommendation

A: the plain triangle between ring and rate. It is understood at once, stays readable in every state, and costs 20 px. C is the same idea made clickable, worth it if other data warnings are to come. E is the one to pick if you want the word rather than a symbol.
