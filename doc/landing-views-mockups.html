RulesABCDEFRecommendation

What every variant keeps

  • Drawings, never data. Teleplot shows what was sent and invents nothing: the previews are the Help's pictures (the same drawings as its gallery, from helpPicture), and "Waiting for data" stays written, so no picture passes for a curve of yours.
  • Each view with what makes it. The line of the text protocol under the picture (or one click away), and a click opens the Help on it, in the language chosen on the left.
  • The first telemetry still brings the Live dashboard, which takes the place of the page (all but C, which proposes a change).
  • Pictures, names and lines come from HELP_TILES: the landing never has its own copy to keep up to date.

the Help's pictures and tiles: nothing new to drawa click opens the sheetlong column

The right column is the Help's gallery, shorter: four families, four tiles each, each with its picture, its name and the line that makes it. "Waiting for data" stays as a line at the top, so nobody takes a picture for their data.

Recommendation

A, the gallery: it is the Help's own gallery (same pictures, same tiles, same click), so it costs almost nothing to build and stays right when the Help changes; it says plainly "waiting for data" above examples that look like the Help, not like a dashboard. D is the best teacher (the shape of a message decides the view) and could be A's grouping: families named by what you send instead of "Numbers, Text…".

B and C look the most like Teleplot, which is also their risk: a picture of a dashboard can pass for one. C is the most useful in use (you see your data land among the examples), but it changes how the Live dashboard comes, not only the landing.
