Landing page, the logo intact: 7 studies

Rules 1234567 Recommendation

The logo stays exactly as it is, its name included: it is the file of the webapp (media/logo-color.svg), shown at its own proportions. What the page tells is put beside it: the sources next to its rings, an actual chart view next to its curve. Each study is a storyboard (1 at rest, 2 something happens, 3 after) with what the device sent, as in landing-mockups.html.

Rules

  • The logo is never touchedThe file itself: not redrawn, recolored, cropped, stretched, animated or covered. Its name stays in it.
  • Room around itNothing closer than the diameter of one of its rings. A leader line may point at a ring or at the end of the curve, and stops before it.
  • Left of it, the sourcesWhere data can come from (from your code, a serial port, a file, the sample), each with its state; the one line to send under the first.
  • Right of it, a real chart viewThe view the library draws, empty and saying so ("Waiting for data") until the first sample: never a curve that looks like data.
  • It hands overThe chart view next to the logo is the first view of the Live dashboard: when data flows, it is the one that stays.
  • Narrow screensThe same pieces stacked: the sources above the logo, the chart under it.
  • Dark themeThe file has white outlines and a slate name: on a dark page the outlines show and the name hardly reads (switch the theme above; the landing of today has the same problem). Kept intact, it needs a light plate behind it, or a dark version of the file, made from it by its author.
